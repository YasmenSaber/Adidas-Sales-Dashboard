## 🚀 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Adidas US sales data** for 2020–2021 using **Power BI**. The goal was to transform raw transactional data into an executive-level, interactive dashboard delivering actionable business insights across multiple dimensions.

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Tool | Power BI Desktop |
| Dataset | Adidas US Sales (2020–2021) |
| Pages | Executive Overview · Product Performance |
| Focus | Revenue, Profitability, Products, Regions, Sales Channels |

## 💡 Key Insights

- 💰 Total Revenue: **900M** | Operating Profit: **332M** (37% margin)
- 👟 **Men's Street Footwear** is the best-performing product by both total sales and operating profit
- 🏬 **In-store** is the dominant sales channel, generating **$356.6M (39.63%)** of total sales
- 📉 **Online sales** remain the weakest channel, highlighting an opportunity for digital growth
- 📊 **Women's Athletic Footwear** contributes only **11.85%** of total sales — the lowest-performing product
- 📈 Sales peak during **Q3**, highlighting seasonal demand and the need for inventory planning
- 🌎 **West** region leads in total sales across all regions

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI Desktop** — Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive visuals
- **DAX** — Calculated KPIs (Operating Margin, Average Profit per Unit, etc.)
- **Interactive Filters** — Date, Region, Sales Method, Product
- **Visualizations** — Treemap, Scatter plot, Donut chart, Line chart, Stacked bar chart

3. Use the slicers on the left to filter by Date / Region / Sales Method / Product
4. All charts and KPIs update dynamically
